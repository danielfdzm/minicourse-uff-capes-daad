\begin{frame}[c]{Make the numerical experiments reproducible}
\small
\begin{slideitems}
\item \textbf{Record the setup:} architecture, activation, precision, random
seed, optimizer settings, and training budget.
\item \textbf{Separate training and evaluation:} assess the learned solution
on independent points, including boundaries and difficult regions.
\item \textbf{Compare the same quantities:} use the same evaluation norms,
reference solution, and evaluation grid for every method.
\item \textbf{Check variability:} repeat with several seeds and report both
typical errors and the spread of the results.
\end{slideitems}
\begin{block}{Try changing one setting}
Change one ingredient at a time: the sampling density, activation, or
optimizer. Keep the other settings fixed and track solution error as well
as the training objective.
\end{block}
\end{frame}
